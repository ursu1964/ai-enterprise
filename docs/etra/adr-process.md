# Architecture Decision Record Process

An ADR is required for domain-boundary, public-contract, persistence, security, deployment,
technology, compatibility, or irreversible operational decisions. Copy `adr-template.md`, allocate
the next four-digit ID, and open it with the implementation. Status moves proposed → accepted →
superseded/deprecated. The architecture owner and affected security/data/service owners approve it.
Exceptions include owner and expiry. CI verifies required sections; reviewers verify evidence.

Accepted ADRs are immutable except status and supersession links. Reversal creates a new ADR. The
decision records context, choice, alternatives, consequences, constitutional principles, migration,
compatibility, security, observability, verification, rollback, and references.
