# Enterprise Evolution Operations Runbook

Owner: Enterprise Governance Board. Run quarterly and after material incidents or strategy changes.

## Assessment

Freeze the evidence window, verify immutable source hashes, run the evolution verifier twice, and
compare evidence hashes. Investigate maturity or benchmark changes before creating proposals.

## Review and approval

Confirm proposer/approver separation, capability transition legality, investment owner, dependencies,
success measures, work-package decomposition, and rollback. Benchmark output is advisory. Record the
human decision through constitutional governance.

## Refactoring and recovery

Execute approved work packages through normal requirements, architecture, implementation, review,
and integration gates. Stop at the first failed measure. Restore the prior specification/generator/
configuration tuple, reconcile lineage, invalidate downstream evidence, and re-enter review.

## Reflection

Publish recommendations with cited evidence and roadmap linkage. Never convert a reflection result
directly into policy, authority, code, deployment, or enterprise-standard capability.
