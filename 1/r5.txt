

R5 — Universal Manifest Transformation Engine (UMTE)

Version: 1.0

Status: Core AI-Enterprise Specification
Purpose

R1 established the Universal Manifest.

R2 defined the Universal Project Language (UPDL).

R3 defined the Project Knowledge Graph.

R4 defined how AI may participate safely.

R5 defines the engine that transforms one Manifest into every artifact required during software creation.

This is one of the most important parts of AI-Enterprise.

The system never asks AI to invent a project.

Instead, AI performs deterministic transformations over structured knowledge.

The Manifest becomes executable.
Core Principle

Everything produced by AI must be a transformation.

Never an invention.

Layer 1 — Semantic Validation

Purpose

Verify the Manifest is internally consistent.

Checks include:

    duplicate identifiers

    undefined references

    invalid actor links

    impossible workflows

    invalid permissions

    missing lifecycle states

    cyclic ownership

    incomplete business rules

No artifact generation occurs until validation succeeds.

AI Boundaries

Within the UMTE, AI may:

    interpret normalized semantics

    execute Registry transformations

    instantiate templates

    generate implementation details constrained by rules

    explain generated artifacts

AI may not:

    invent new business objects

    modify approved business rules

    change identifiers

    alter workflows

    introduce undocumented dependencies

    bypass validation or verification

Architectural Outcome

With R5, AI-Enterprise transitions from a passive documentation framework to an executable engineering platform. The Manifest is no longer just a specification—it becomes the single source from which every software artifact is deterministically derived. AI's role is confined to controlled, traceable transformations governed by the Registry and the Knowledge Graph, ensuring consistency, reproducibility, and complete lifecycle synchronization across every generated deliverable.
