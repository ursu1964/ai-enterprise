# Deterministic and AI boundary v0.1

Structural authority belongs to deterministic application code.

The deterministic engine is responsible for schema validation, required-field checks, duplicate
identifier detection, reference integrity, orphan detection, supported status validation, owner
presence, acceptance-criteria presence, state-transition validation, traceability coverage, and
cardinality rules.

AI interpretation is limited to semantic assistance: extracting candidate fields from prose,
suggesting semantic duplicates, detecting ambiguity, drafting candidate requirements, identifying
likely contradictions, drafting clarification questions, and suggesting classifications.

An AI operation must never be the final authority for structural validity, approval, snapshot
eligibility, or artifact consistency. AI-created or AI-modified material must remain reviewable,
hash-bound, and marked as proposed, inferred, or unverified until a human review decision changes
its approval status.
